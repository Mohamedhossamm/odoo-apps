Arabic Amount in Words (Tafqeet)

Print the invoice total in correct Arabic words, the way accountants write it:

فقط ألف ومائتان وأربعة وثلاثون جنيهاً وخمسون قرشاً لا غير

Why Odoo's own text is not enough

Odoo writes amounts with a generic library that ignores Arabic number grammar. It writes 2,000,000 as "مليونا" instead of "مليونان", "خمسون قرش" without the accusative, puts a space after every "و", and knows nothing about the currency noun that must follow the number.

This app replaces it with a grammar engine built for Arabic financial documents:

NumberForm of the currencyExample
1noun + واحدجنيه واحد / هللة واحدة
2dualجنيهان / هللتان
3 – 10plural, opposite genderثلاثة جنيهات / ثلاث هللات
11 – 99singular accusativeأحد عشر جنيهاً
100, 1,000, …singular genitiveمائة جنيه / ألفا جنيه

Arabic invoices

Invoices printed in Arabic show the corrected text on Odoo's own "amount in words" line.

Bilingual invoices

One setting adds the Arabic amount under the English one on invoices printed in any other language, as required on bilingual invoices in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf.

Features

  • Correct Arabic grammar for 1, 2, 3–10, 11–99 and hundreds, thousands, millions and billions.
  • Masculine and feminine currency nouns: ثلاثة ريالات وثلاث هللات.
  • Currencies: EGP, SAR, AED, QAR, KWD, BHD, JOD, OMR and USD, including three-decimal dinars and rials.
  • Optional "فقط … لا غير" wording.
  • Works wherever Odoo writes amounts in words in Arabic, including cheque printing.
  • Other currencies and languages keep Odoo's standard text.

Setup

  1. Install the app.
  2. In Accounting › Configuration › Settings, enable Total amount of invoice in letters.
  3. Optionally enable Arabic amount in words on all invoices for bilingual invoices.

Compatibility

  • Odoo 17, tested on Community edition.
  • Depends only on Invoicing (account).
