POS Arabic & English Receipt

Bilingual Point of Sale receipts for Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf.

إيصال كاشير بالعربي والإنجليزي

What it adds to the receipt

  • Arabic product names printed under the English name, taken from the product's Arabic translation.
  • The total in Arabic words: فقط أربعمائة وستون دولاراً لا غير. Computed in the browser, so it works offline.
  • Bilingual labels: TOTAL / الإجمالي, CHANGE / الباقي, Discounts / الخصومات, Rounding / التقريب.
  • The customer's name and phone when the order has a customer.

Correct Arabic grammar

The amount in words follows Arabic number grammar: the dual (جنيهان), the plural for 3 to 10 (ثلاثة جنيهات), the accusative for 11 to 99 (أحد عشر جنيهاً) and feminine currency nouns (ثلاث هللات).

Currencies: EGP, SAR, AED, QAR, KWD, BHD, JOD, OMR and USD, including three-decimal dinars and rials. Other currencies print no words.

Setup

  1. Install the app and activate the Arabic language.
  2. Translate product names into Arabic (the language icon on the product name field).
  3. Each part can be turned off in Point of Sale › Configuration › Settings › Bills & Receipts.

A POS used in Arabic does not repeat Arabic text that is already on the receipt.

Compatibility

  • Odoo 17, tested on Community edition.
  • Depends only on Point of Sale.
